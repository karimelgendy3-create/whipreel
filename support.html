Common questions

How do I restore my purchases?

In the app, open Settings and tap Restore purchases, or tap Restore at the top of the plans screen. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. In the app, go to Settings, Your plan, Cancel subscription. Or open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Whipreel. Your plan stays on until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do credits expire?

Credits you buy never expire. Credits that come with Pro+ reset every week or month, depending on your plan, and don't roll over.

My edit failed. Did I lose my credits?

No. If an edit fails, its credits are returned to you automatically.

Which photos work best?

Photos with the whole car in the picture, in daylight, from the angles the app asks for: front, front corner, side, rear corner and rear. The app sorts your photos by angle for you, and you can fix any of them.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes everything saved on your phone. To delete photos or edits from our servers, email us. See the Privacy Policy for details.
