Whipreel

Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

Whipreel is an iPhone app that turns photos of your car into short video edits. It is made by the developer shown on its App Store page ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and who it is shared with. If you have a question, email whipreelsupport@gmail.com.

The short version
  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your photos are uploaded only to make your edits, and only after you say yes.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

What the app collects

Photos and clips you add

When you add photos or clips of your car, the app uploads them to our servers to sort them by angle, read your car's make, model and colour, and make your edits. The first time, the app asks you before anything is uploaded. If you say no, nothing leaves your phone, but edits can't be made.

Your car's details

The make, model and colour read from your photos (you can change them) are sent with your photos, so they can appear in your edits and captions.

Purchases

Apple handles payment; we never see your card or billing details. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and unlock your plan. It receives your purchase history and a random ID the app creates, which isn't tied to your name or email.

Basic technical information

When the app talks to our servers and service providers, basic technical information is sent with the request, such as your iOS version and app version, for example when the app checks for an update.

We don't collect your location, contacts, browsing history or any identifier used for advertising.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to sort your photos, make and deliver your edits, unlock what you've paid for, and keep the service working. We don't use your data for advertising and we don't sell it.

Who we share it with

Only with the service providers that help run Whipreel, and only for that job:

  • Google (through OpenRouter): analyses small copies of your photos to sort them by angle and read your car's details, and checks Premium edits before they're delivered.
  • BytePlus (a ByteDance company): renders Premium video edits from your photos, with number plates blurred first.
  • RevenueCat: confirms purchases and manages subscriptions.
  • Apple: payments and app distribution.
  • Expo: delivers app updates.

These providers may process data in the United States and other countries. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Your cars, photos and edits are saved on your phone, and deleting the app deletes them. Photos and clips uploaded to make an edit, and the finished edits, are stored on our servers until you ask us to delete them: we don't delete them automatically yet. Email us and we'll delete them. If we set a fixed period later, this policy will say so.

Your choices and rights

  • You can decline the upload prompt. Nothing is uploaded until you agree.
  • To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email whipreelsupport@gmail.com. As there are no accounts, tell us roughly when you made your edits so we can find them.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), you have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your data. We honour these requests for everyone.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and kept on systems only we and our providers can access.

Children

Whipreel isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
